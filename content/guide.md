**Example, not for publication.** These words are placeholder drafting for the
build, and no chaplain review stands behind them. The record is held in review,
so only the beta build shows this page: the production build offers no
younger-child path at all until a chaplain has read a real guide.

## How to offer one of these

Offer one thing, not a list. Say once that it is optional, and then let it be.

"No" is a complete answer. So is silence, and so is falling asleep.

You do not have to ask afterwards whether it helped. Nothing here is waiting on
an answer from either of you.

## How to adapt one

Keep the heart of it. Change the size, the words, and what the hands are doing.

Play is a child's version of most of this, and so is doing the same small thing
again and again.

Follow the care team on touch, on materials, and on anything that leaves the
room.

## This is for you as well

Everything here is also for you, as yourself, and not only as someone holding it
for a child.

You can use any of it on your own, and nothing changes when you do. You do not
have to be here on someone else's behalf.

## What this cannot tell you

This resource has never met your child.

It cannot say which practice suits which age, and nothing here is marked as
suitable for one. That judgement is yours, with the people caring for them.
