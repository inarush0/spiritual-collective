# Spiritual Care Resource

This context describes a shareable spiritual-care resource for pediatric hospice and palliative-care settings. It supports personal exploration without replacing professional spiritual, medical, mental-health, or emergency care.

## Language

**Spiritual practice**:
An optional, adaptable activity that may support awareness, connection, meaning, compassion, devotion, or relationship with what a person considers sacred.
_Avoid_: Prescription, treatment

**Pathway**:
A non-exclusive orientation that helps a person discover spiritual practices that may fit what they are drawn to in the present moment.
_Avoid_: Category, diagnosis, result

**Practice record**:
The complete, reviewable description of one spiritual practice, every field of which must be filled before that practice may be published.
_Avoid_: Entry, article, page

**Content standard**:
The fixed set of practice-record fields plus the plain-language checklist that every practice record is measured against before publication.
_Avoid_: Style guide, template

**Need tag**:
A state a person may recognize in themselves, carried by every practice record so that practices can be found by what someone is living through rather than by what kind of practice they are.
_Avoid_: Mood, symptom, goal

**Smallest version**:
The least a person can do and still have done the practice, offered so that nothing carries a duration target or completion pressure.
_Avoid_: Duration, minimum time, quick option

**Direct user**:
An adolescent receiving pediatric hospice or palliative care, or an adult family member, who explores the resource independently or alongside a chaplain.
_Avoid_: Patient, when the intended group also includes family members

**Chaplain reviewer**:
The single chaplain who holds the veto on publishing any user-facing wording, and whose approval no one may overrule or grant on their behalf.
_Avoid_: Administrator, author, owner of the catalog

**Editor**:
The person who composes practice records and decides what is proposed, who may remove published content alone but may never publish without the chaplain reviewer's approval.
_Avoid_: Author, when the point is the authority to publish rather than the writing

**Review record**:
The part of a practice record holding who attested which gates, on what date, against which version of that practice, so that an approval can be located and trusted long after it was given.
_Avoid_: Sign-off, audit log

**Beta release**:
The unlisted, uncrawlable copy of the resource where pending content is met as a direct user would meet it, so that review can judge the experience and not only the wording.
_Avoid_: Staging, draft site, preview

**Promotion**:
The movement of one approved practice record into the production release, granted per record so that a practice held back never delays the rest.
_Avoid_: Deploy, launch, release, when a single record rather than the site is meant

**Editorial correction**:
A change that cannot alter what a person does or how safe it is, which is logged and leaves the existing approval standing.
_Avoid_: Minor edit, typo fix, when the size of the change rather than its effect on the reader is meant

**Substantive change**:
Any change that could alter what a person does or how safe it is, which voids the approval and returns the practice record to review.
_Avoid_: Major edit, rewrite

**Withdrawal**:
The immediate removal of one published practice from the resource, available without approval because leaving harmful content up is the greater risk.
_Avoid_: Rollback, delete, unpublish, when the whole site rather than one practice is meant

**Seed material**:
The two authorized source documents whose spiritual-practice framework and definitions may be adapted, challenged, or omitted without public attribution to their author.
_Avoid_: Canonical content, approved content
